# Sales Performance Dashboard
## Project Objective
To analyze a real-world sales dataset using Power BI to identify sales trends, top-performing products, customer purchasing behavior, and regional sales performance, and to present actionable business insights through an interactive dashboard.

## Questions (KPIs)
- What is the total sales generated by the business?
- What is the total profit earned from the sales?
- How many total orders were received?
- What is the total quantity of products sold?

## Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to analyze sales data using Power BI and answer important business questions such as:

What is the total sales generated?
What is the total profit earned?
How many total orders were placed?
What is the total quantity sold?
How do sales change over time?
Which categories perform better?
Which regions generate higher sales?
Which subcategories perform better?
How does customer sales performance vary?

The dashboard includes the following key performance indicators:
